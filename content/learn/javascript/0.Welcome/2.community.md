# Join the Community

Learning JavaScript gets easier (and a lot more fun) when you're not doing it alone. 🤝

Think about how you learned cycling as a kid. 🚲 You probably didn't read a book about it. Someone held the back of your seat, cheered when you got it right, and helped you up when you fell. Learning to code works the same way.

AiroByte has a growing community of learners just like you. Here's how you can be part of it.

## 📱 Follow Our WhatsApp Channel

Our **WhatsApp channel** is the easiest way to stay connected. You already open WhatsApp every day, so learning becomes part of your routine without any extra app.

When you follow the channel, you'll get:

- 🆕 **New lessons** as soon as they are published
- 💡 **Quick JavaScript tips** you can read in under a minute
- 🧩 **Practice problems** to test what you've learned
- 🎯 **Interview questions** to help you prepare for your next job
- 📢 **Updates** about new courses and features on AiroByte

[**👉 Follow AiroByte on WhatsApp**](https://whatsapp.com/channel/0029VbDstLG3WHTXMi2nDp29){target="_blank"}

> 💡 **Tip:** After you follow the channel, tap the 🔔 bell icon so you get a notification whenever we post something new.

## 💬 Share Your Feedback

We're working hard to improve the lessons **every day**, and your feedback is the best guide we have.

Tell us when:

- 🤔 Something feels **unclear** or confusing
- 🕳️ A topic or example is **missing**
- 🐛 You find a **mistake** in a lesson or a code example
- ✨ You have an **idea** that could make learning here better

Every message helps us make **AiroByte the best place to learn JavaScript and prepare for interviews**.

## 🌱 Make the Most of Learning Together

- **Learn a little every day.** 20 minutes daily beats 5 hours once a week.
- **Try every code example** in the playground. Don't just read it, run it and change it!
- **Teach what you learn.** Explaining a topic to a friend is the fastest way to truly understand it.
- **Don't fear mistakes.** Every error message is a lesson in disguise. 🎁
